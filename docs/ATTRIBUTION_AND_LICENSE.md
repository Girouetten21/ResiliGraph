**ResiliGraph** is an open-source project created to advance resilient distributed systems engineering, interactive architectural simulation, and living system specifications.

## ⚖️ Attribution & Commercial Use Policy

While the project is open source and freely accessible to everyone, **we kindly and strictly enforce the following attribution requirement**:

> **Notice on Commercial Redistribution & Sales:**
> You are free to modify, extend, and adapt ResiliGraph. However, **you may NOT sell, redistribute as a commercial product, or re-package this project or its derivative works without giving clear, visible, and prominent prior credit and attribution to ResiliGraph and its original creators.**

### What Constitutes Proper Attribution?

If you include ResiliGraph or any substantial portion of its simulation engine, canvas architecture, or design system in a public project, commercial service, presentation, or product:

1. **Prominent Credit in Documentation & UI:**
   - Display a visible attribution notice:
     ```
     Powered by ResiliGraph (Interactive Distributed Architecture Simulator)
     Original Project: https://github.com/Girouetten21/ResiliGraph
     Author: Girouetten21
     ```
2. **Preserve Copyright & License Notices:**
   - Retain the original copyright notices and license text in all copies or substantial portions of the Software.
3. **No False Representation:**
   - Do not claim sole authorship or ownership of the core simulation engine or architectural design system.
